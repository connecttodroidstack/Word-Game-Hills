This Privacy Policy explains how Word Game Hills (“the game”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our Android app. We keep things simple: the game is built to work without an account and without collecting personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location. The player name you can type on the Profile screen is stored only on your device.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, gems, collection items and settings are saved locally on your device. If Android’s backup is turned on, Android may store a copy in your own Google account backup so your progress can be restored after reinstalling. You can also copy a backup code from the game’s Settings. We cannot see or access this data.

3. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to improve it, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects anonymous usage information such as: levels started and finished, time taken, hints used, app version, device model, operating system version and approximate country (from the IP address). This is linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to your name. We do not collect the advertising ID for analytics and ad-personalisation signals are turned off. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

3a. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details only: what went wrong in the code, the app version, device model, operating system version and a random installation identifier. It does not include your name or anything you type.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the Home screen (never during puzzles) and optional rewarded video ads that you only see if you choose to tap “Watch Ad”. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address and approximate location. Ads are limited to content rated PG (suitable for general audiences), and we block ad categories such as alcohol, gambling and dating. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Settings > Privacy > Ads.

4a. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland

Before any ads are requested, the game shows Google’s consent message so you can agree to, or refuse, personalised ads and the use of your data by Google and its ad partners (GDPR). If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices (ads).

4b. Players in the United States

Some US states (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut and Utah) give you the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell your personal information for money, but sharing device identifiers with Google to show personalised ads may count as “sharing” under these laws. To opt out, open the game and go to Settings > Privacy choices (ads) and choose Do not sell or share my personal information. After opting out you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

5. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Coins and gems are earned by playing and have no real-money value.

6. Children’s privacy

The game’s content is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data security & retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game. Analytics data is kept by Google for the retention period set in our Firebase project (up to 14 months) and then deleted automatically.

8. Your choices & deleting your data

  • Use Settings > Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game, to delete your game data.
  • To request deletion of analytics data linked to your device, email us (see below).
  • You can turn off Android backup in your device settings at any time.
  • To change ad consent or opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information, use Settings > Privacy choices (ads) in the game.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version.
