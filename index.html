🏔️ A relaxing word adventure

Word Game Hills is a calm, colourful word puzzle game for all ages. Swipe across the letter wheel to spell words, fill the crossword grid and climb level by level through meadows, forests, waterfalls and snowy peaks. Learn the meaning of every word you find, collect mountain animals and plants, and play anywhere — no internet needed.
